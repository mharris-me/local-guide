Freedom Park

This park is one of the largest in the city and offers a variety of space for recreational activities. This park is known for its open space and it beautiful view and is one of the most popular parks in Charlotte city.

The Green

Visit the 1.5 acre park with it's very unique literary features of book sculptures, gardens, and landscapes! There is plenty of space for sunny outdoor activites for people of all kinds!

Independence Park

The nice and peaceful atmosphere makes Independence Park a great place to relax and enjoy nature. This park is located below street traffic which makes for a nice and quiet environment for a day of reading and relaxation.
